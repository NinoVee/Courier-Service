Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for busy healthcare teams

What is a STAT medical courier delivery?
A STAT delivery is our most urgent service tier: a dedicated courier is dispatched directly to your facility, typically within 15–30 minutes, and drives your item point-to-point without intermediate stops.
Do you serve all of Southern California?
Yes. We provide daily scheduled routes and 24/7 STAT coverage across Los Angeles, Orange, San Bernardino, Riverside, and Ventura counties.
Is Thompson Medical Logistics HIPAA compliant?
Yes. All drivers and dispatch staff complete HIPAA training, and we execute Business Associate Agreements with applicable healthcare clients.
How do you maintain chain of custody for specimens?
Every pickup and drop-off is scan-verified and time-stamped, with electronic signatures captured at each handoff so the custody trail is fully documented.
Can you handle temperature-sensitive shipments?
Yes. We use validated cold-chain containers with continuous temperature logging for refrigerated, frozen, and cryogenic transport.
What is your average response time for STAT requests?
Our average STAT response time across Southern California is approximately 27 minutes from dispatch to courier arrival, depending on traffic and location.
Do you offer scheduled, recurring routes?
Yes. Many of our hospital, lab, and clinic partners run daily or multiple-times-daily standing routes on a set schedule we build around their workflow.
Are your drivers background checked?
Yes. Every driver completes a background check, DMV screening, and drug-free workplace testing before joining our fleet.
Can I track my delivery in real time?
Yes. Every shipment is tracked via GPS from pickup to delivery, and you'll receive real-time status notifications and an electronic proof of delivery.
Do you transport pharmaceuticals?
Yes, including time- and temperature-sensitive medications delivered to pharmacies, infusion centers, and patient homes.
Do you work with veterinary hospitals?
Yes. We support veterinary laboratories and hospitals with specimen transport and supply delivery using the same chain-of-custody standards as our human healthcare clients.
What service types can I select when requesting a quote?
You can choose STAT, same-day scheduled, or recurring route service, and specify frequency — daily, weekly, or custom — on our quote request form.
How quickly can I get a service quote?
Most quote requests submitted through our online form receive a response from our team within one business hour during standard operations.
Do you provide proof of delivery documentation?
Yes. Every delivery includes an electronic proof of delivery with timestamp, GPS location, and signature, accessible through our client portal.
Who do I contact for after-hours emergencies?
Our emergency dispatch line is staffed 24/7 at (714) 555-0911 for urgent, after-hours courier needs.
