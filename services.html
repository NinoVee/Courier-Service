Our Services

Medical courier services built for every handoff in healthcare

From routine specimen loops to life-critical STAT runs, each service is run to the handling standard the material requires.

STAT Deliveries

When timing is critical, STAT service puts a dedicated courier on the road directly to your facility.

  • Average dispatch-to-arrival of 27 minutes
  • Direct point-to-point routing, no shared stops
  • Priority phone line to live dispatch
  • Ideal for time-critical labs, blood products, and organs

How It Works

From request to signed delivery, in four steps

Step 01

Request Service

Submit a quote request or call dispatch with your pickup and delivery details.

Step 02

Courier Dispatched

A trained courier is assigned and routed to your pickup location.

Step 03

Tracked In Transit

GPS location and, where applicable, temperature are logged the entire route.

Step 04

Signed & Confirmed

Electronic proof of delivery is captured and available in your portal instantly.
