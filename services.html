About Thompson Medical Logistics

Built by logistics professionals who understand what healthcare can’t afford to get wrong

Every specimen, chart, and dose we carry represents a patient waiting on the other end. That responsibility shapes everything about how we operate.

Our Story

A regional courier built specifically for healthcare logistics

Thompson Medical Logistics was founded to solve a problem general couriers weren’t built to handle: the precision, urgency, and compliance that healthcare transport demands. What began as a small fleet serving independent laboratories across Orange County has grown into a full-service medical logistics operation covering all of Southern California.

Today we support hospitals, reference laboratories, surgery centers, dialysis clinics, urgent care networks, pharmacies, veterinary hospitals, and home health agencies — each with service protocols built around their specific handling, timing, and documentation requirements.

We didn’t set out to be the biggest courier in the region. We set out to be the one hospitals and labs never have to think twice about.
