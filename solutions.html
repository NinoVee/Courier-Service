Healthcare Solutions

Logistics solutions tailored to how your organization actually operates

Every healthcare setting moves differently. We build service plans around your patients, your volume, and your compliance requirements.

Hospitals

Multi-campus hospital systems rely on Thompson for inter-facility transfers, STAT specimen runs, pharmacy delivery, and medical records courier — coordinated across departments and shifts.

  • Inter-campus transfer routes
  • STAT lab and blood bank runs
  • Pharmacy and central supply delivery

Clinics

Outpatient and multi-location clinic groups use scheduled routes to move specimens and supplies between exam rooms, labs, and partner facilities without disrupting patient flow.

  • Daily scheduled specimen pickup
  • Multi-location route coordination
  • Same-day supply replenishment

Reference Labs

Independent and reference laboratories depend on us for high-volume specimen logistics with strict chain-of-custody and turnaround requirements.

  • High-volume daily specimen routes
  • STAT courier for critical results
  • Scan-verified chain of custody

Urgent Care

Urgent care networks need fast, flexible courier support for walk-in volume that doesn’t follow a predictable schedule.

  • On-demand STAT dispatch
  • Multi-site network coverage
  • Rapid specimen transport to labs

Dialysis

Dialysis centers trust Thompson for time-sensitive lab work and supply delivery that keeps treatment schedules on track.

  • Time-critical lab specimen transport
  • Recurring supply delivery routes
  • Coordination around treatment schedules

Surgery Centers

Ambulatory surgery centers use our white-glove equipment and instrument tray transport to keep procedure schedules moving.

  • Surgical instrument tray transport
  • Loaner equipment delivery and pickup
  • STAT pathology specimen runs

Veterinary

Veterinary hospitals and labs receive the same chain-of-custody standard as our human healthcare clients for specimen and supply transport.

  • Veterinary specimen transport
  • Scheduled supply delivery
  • STAT dispatch for urgent cases

Tell us about your organization — we’ll design the route plan around it.
